How to use them

Fasten the strap round your wrist, lay the rubber pad over the bar and close your grip on top. No wrapping or threading, so they're quick between sets.

Care

Hand wash only. Rinse the rubber with water now and then to keep it grippy, and keep the Velcro closed when you're not using them.

Allergy note

The synthetic rubber is made where natural rubber is also handled. Not recommended if you're sensitive to latex or rubber.
